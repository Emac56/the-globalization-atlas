What is Globalization?

Globalization is the growing connection between countries, economies, cultures, and people around the world. It happens through trade, technology, migration, and communication.

In simple words

The world is getting closer together, so what happens in one place can affect people far away.

Key characteristics

Tap a card to read more.

A change in one place, such as prices, rules, or news, can quickly affect people elsewhere.

Travel, migration, trade, and online services make these movements faster and more common.

Internet and mobile tools let people talk and share information instantly, wherever they are.

Everyday Example

A video call with a relative working abroad

Why it counts as globalization

A simple family call connects people in different countries through the internet, work abroad, money sent home, and shared culture. Ordinary daily life is tied to the wider world.
