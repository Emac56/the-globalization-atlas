Connection Notes

Countries Involved

  • Côte d’Ivoire: Exports cocoa beans.
  • Malaysia: Processes cocoa and makes chocolate products.
  • Philippines: Imports and sells cocoa-containing products.

Who Benefits?

  • Cocoa farmers earn income.
  • Malaysian processors make cocoa products.
  • Philippine importers and retailers sell products.
  • Filipino consumers can buy chocolate.

Problems

  • Child Labor: Reported in cocoa farming in Côte d’Ivoire and Ghana.
  • Supply Issues: Weather and crop problems can affect cocoa production.
  • Traceability: Tracking cocoa’s origin can be difficult.

How Are They Connected?

  • Côte d’Ivoire exports cocoa beans to Malaysia.
  • Malaysia processes cocoa and makes chocolate products.
  • The Philippines imports cocoa-containing products from Malaysia.
  • These trade links connect the countries through the cocoa supply chain.

Note: Trade data does not prove that every chocolate product imported from Malaysia uses cocoa from Côte d’Ivoire.
