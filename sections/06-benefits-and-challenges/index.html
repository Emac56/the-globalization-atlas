Benefit 01

More Jobs in the Philippines

Why it happens

Globalization opens employment opportunities through international trade and foreign investment.

Who benefits

Filipino workers and their families.

Who is affected

Workers who lack the skills needed for modern jobs.

Concrete example

The International Labour Organization explains that trade policies affect employment in the Philippines.

Benefit 02

Access to New Technology

Why it happens

Globalization makes it easier for Philippine businesses to access digital tools, modern equipment, and new knowledge.

Who benefits

Filipino businesses, workers, students, and consumers.

Who is affected

Businesses and workers without access to technology or digital skills.

Concrete example

The World Bank identifies technology, media, and telecommunications as important sectors for Philippine participation in global value chains.

Benefit 03

Wider Market for Filipino Businesses

Why it happens

Globalization allows Filipino companies to sell products and services to customers in other countries.

Who benefits

Filipino entrepreneurs, exporters, workers, and consumers.

Who is affected

Local businesses that cannot compete with foreign companies.

Concrete example

The World Bank says deeper participation in global value chains can strengthen the competitiveness of the Philippine economy.

Benefit 04

Cultural Exchange

Why it happens

Globalization allows Filipinos to learn about other cultures through media, travel, food, music, and online platforms.

Who benefits

Filipino students, fans, artists, and communities.

Who is affected

Local traditions may be influenced if foreign culture becomes too dominant.

Concrete example

In 2023, 13 Filipino dance groups joined a K-pop event in Manila, showing cultural exchange between Filipinos and Koreans.

Challenge 01

Job Competition

Why it happens

Filipino workers compete with workers from other countries, so they need updated skills.

Who benefits

Skilled workers and companies that can adapt.

Who is affected

Workers without digital or technical skills.

Concrete example

The World Bank says the Philippines needs stronger skills development because many workers lack the skills demanded by businesses.

Challenge 02

Environmental Problems

Why it happens

More production, transportation, and trade can cause pollution and damage Philippine natural resources.

Who benefits

Businesses that earn more from increased production and trade.

Who is affected

Filipino communities near factories, ports, and roads.

Concrete example

The World Bank's environmental analysis examines environmental problems and the effects of pollution and resource management in the Philippines.

Challenge 03

Economic Dependence

Why it happens

The Philippines may depend on imports, foreign investment, exports, and global supply chains.

Who benefits

Businesses connected to global markets during stable economic conditions.

Who is affected

Filipino workers, businesses, and consumers during global disruptions.

Concrete example

The World Bank explains that global value chains connect the Philippines to other countries but also expose it to global disruptions.

Challenge 04

Cultural Changes

Why it happens

Foreign media, languages, products, and lifestyles can influence Philippine culture and traditions.

Who benefits

People who enjoy global entertainment, products, and ideas.

Who is affected

Local communities, Indigenous groups, and speakers of Philippine languages.

Concrete example

The United Nations highlights the need to protect cultural and linguistic diversity as globalization spreads.
