More Jobs in the Philippines

Why it happens

Globalization connects the Philippines to international trade and foreign companies, which can create more jobs.

Who benefits

Filipino workers and their families benefit through income and opportunities to develop new skills.

Who is affected

Workers who gain employment benefit from new opportunities, while workers who lack the required skills may have fewer employment opportunities.

Concrete example

The International Labour Organization explains that trade policies affect employment in the Philippines.

Job Competition Among Filipino Workers

Why it happens

Globalization connects workers and businesses across countries, while technological changes increase the need for updated skills.

Who benefits

Skilled workers and companies that adapt to changing technologies and international demands may gain better opportunities.

Who is affected

Filipino workers who lack updated technical or digital skills may have difficulty finding jobs or competing for better employment opportunities.

Concrete example

The World Bank's Philippines Country Growth and Jobs Report 2025 discusses the importance of developing workforce skills to support better employment and economic growth.

Access to New Technology

Why it happens

Globalization gives Philippine businesses access to digital tools, modern equipment, and knowledge from other countries.

Who benefits

Filipino workers, students, businesses, and consumers benefit from access to new technology.

Who is affected

Businesses and workers without access to modern technology or digital skills may struggle to keep up with technological changes.

Concrete example

The World Bank identifies technology, media, and telecommunications as important areas for Philippine participation in global value chains.

Environmental Problems

Why it happens

Increased production, shipping, and trade can contribute to pollution and place pressure on Philippine natural resources.

Who benefits

Businesses may earn more from increased production and trade, while consumers gain access to more products.

Who is affected

Filipino communities near factories, ports, and major roads may experience pollution and environmental damage.

Concrete example

The World Bank's environmental analysis of the Philippines examines environmental concerns and the effects of pollution and resource management on the country.

Wider Market for Filipino Businesses

Why it happens

Globalization allows Filipino companies to sell products and services to customers in other countries.

Who benefits

Filipino entrepreneurs, exporters, workers, and consumers can benefit from expanded markets and business opportunities.

Who is affected

Small businesses that struggle to compete with foreign companies may lose customers or find it difficult to expand.

Concrete example

The World Bank explains that deeper participation in global value chains can strengthen the competitiveness of the Philippine economy.

Economic Dependence

Why it happens

The Philippines may depend on imports, foreign investment, exports, and global supply chains to support economic activity.

Who benefits

Businesses connected to global markets may benefit from international trade, investment, and access to foreign customers.

Who is affected

Filipino consumers, workers, and local businesses may experience higher prices, reduced opportunities, or supply shortages when global markets are disrupted.

Concrete example

The World Bank explains how global value chains connect the Philippines to international markets, creating opportunities while exposing the economy to external disruptions.

Cultural Exchange

Why it happens

Globalization allows Filipinos to experience other cultures through media, travel, food, music, and online platforms.

Who benefits

Filipino students, fans, artists, and communities benefit from opportunities to learn about and share different cultures.

Who is affected

Local communities may experience changes in their traditions and cultural practices as foreign influences become more common.

Concrete example

In 2023, Filipino dance groups participated in a K-pop event in Manila, demonstrating cultural exchange between Filipinos and Koreans.

Cultural Changes

Why it happens

Foreign media, languages, products, and lifestyles can influence Philippine culture and traditional practices.

Who benefits

People who enjoy global entertainment, products, and ideas may benefit from having more choices and opportunities to learn about other cultures.

Who is affected

Indigenous communities, local cultural groups, and speakers of Philippine languages may face challenges in preserving their traditions and cultural heritage.

Concrete example

The United Nations highlights the importance of protecting cultural and linguistic diversity as global connections expand.
