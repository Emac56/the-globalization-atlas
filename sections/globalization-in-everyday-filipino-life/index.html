Globalization is not only something that happens between countries. It can also be seen in the everyday lives of Filipinos.

Five everyday examples

Select an example to see how it connects to the world.

Example: McDonald’s in the Philippines

Global Connection

McDonald’s started in the United States and expanded to the Philippines.

Effect on Filipino Life

Filipinos can enjoy an international fast-food brand locally.

Example: UNIQLO in the Philippines

Global Connection

UNIQLO is a Japanese clothing brand with stores in the Philippines.

Effect on Filipino Life

Filipinos can buy clothing from an international brand locally.

Example: Korean dramas and popular culture

Global Connection

Korean media has influenced Philippine popular culture.

Effect on Filipino Life

Korean entertainment influences what Filipinos watch and discuss.

Example: Internet and digital services

Global Connection

The internet connects Filipinos with people, information, and services worldwide.

Effect on Filipino Life

It supports communication, learning, and online business.

Example: Filipinos working abroad

Global Connection

Filipinos work in countries such as Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Singapore.

Effect on Filipino Life

Overseas employment provides income and connects Filipino families to international labor markets through remittances.
